Frances Chi

Senior Partner & Non-Executive Director, Jebsen Consumer

Frances Chi is a Senior Partner and Non-Executive Director at Jebsen Consumer, where she provides strategic advisory support and independent perspectives on corporate governance, long-term business development, strategic partnerships, international brand development, and sustainable value creation across Greater China.

Her professional background spans global commodities trading, business strategy, financial risk management, and corporate governance. During her career at Trafigura, she progressed through Business Analyst, Senior Business Analyst, Senior Manager Business Analysis, and Senior Risk Manager positions, gaining extensive experience in commercial analysis, market exposure, financial risk assessment, and international trading activities.

She holds a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from The Hong Kong Polytechnic University and a Master of Arts in Economics from Stanford University. She also holds the Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) and Financial Risk Manager (FRM) credentials.

Her professional interests include global economic developments, investment strategy, technological innovation, international trade, and long-term business partnerships.
